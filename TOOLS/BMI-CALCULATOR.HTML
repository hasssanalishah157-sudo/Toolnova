What Is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a commonly used screening measurement based on a person's weight in relation to their height. BMI can provide a general indication of whether a person's weight falls within a particular range.

How to Use This BMI Calculator

Enter your weight in kilograms and your height in centimeters. Then click the Calculate BMI button. The calculator converts your height to meters and calculates your BMI automatically.

BMI Formula

BMI is calculated by dividing body weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

General BMI Categories for Adults

BMI Range Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Normal weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or higher Obesity

Important Information

BMI is a screening tool and does not directly measure body fat or overall health. Factors such as muscle mass, age and other individual characteristics can affect how BMI should be interpreted.

For health-related decisions, consider discussing your individual circumstances with a qualified healthcare professional.
