What is a Case Converter?

A Case Converter is an online text formatting tool that changes the capitalization of text without requiring you to type it again. It can quickly convert text into uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case.

Case Converter Features

  • Convert text to UPPERCASE.
  • Convert text to lowercase.
  • Convert text to Title Case.
  • Convert text to Sentence Case.
  • Capitalize the first letter of every word.
  • Copy converted text with one click.
  • Works directly in your browser.

Why Use ToolNova Case Converter?

ToolNova Case Converter is free and easy to use. You can paste text from documents, websites, notes or social media and change its capitalization instantly. No account or installation is required.
