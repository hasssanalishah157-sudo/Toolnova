What is a Discount Calculator?

A Discount Calculator helps you find the amount of money saved after applying a percentage discount to an original price. It also calculates the final sale price.

How to Calculate a Discount

Enter the original price and the discount percentage. The calculator automatically calculates the discount amount and subtracts it from the original price.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount
