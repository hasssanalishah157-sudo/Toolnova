What is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online tool that counts the number of words in a piece of text. ToolNova's free word counter also counts characters, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.

How to Use the Word Counter

Type or paste your text into the text box above. The word, character, sentence and paragraph counts will update automatically as you type.

Who Can Use a Word Counter?

A word counter can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, teachers, content creators and anyone who needs to check the length of their text.

Reading Time Calculator

ToolNova also provides an estimated reading time based on the number of words in your text. This can help writers understand approximately how long their content may take to read.
