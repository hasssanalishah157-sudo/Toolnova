What is a QR Code Generator?

A QR Code Generator is an online tool that converts text, website links, URLs and other information into a scannable QR code.

How to Create a QR Code

Enter your text or website URL in the box above and click the Generate QR Code button. Your QR code will appear instantly.

You can then download the generated QR code as a PNG image and use it on websites, documents, posters, business cards and other materials.

What Can You Use a QR Code For?

QR codes can be used for website links, contact information, text messages, business information, product pages and many other everyday purposes.
